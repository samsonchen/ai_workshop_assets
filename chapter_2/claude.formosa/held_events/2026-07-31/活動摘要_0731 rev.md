Tina Lin

Tina brought a very distinctive topic: using Claude AI to figure out daily outfits.

By telling Claude about the clothes in her wardrobe — including brand information — she has Claude plan out a whole week of outfits in advance. If there's a special occasion coming up, she can ask for something different for that day, and she can even tell Claude which style icon she'd like to emulate.

Here's the prompt Tina uses to have Claude generate a style profile — feel free to try it with your own Claude:

I want to build a weekly scheduled routine that reminds me of personal outfit suggestions. Please act as my personal styling assistant.
Please create a style-profile.md in the folder I specify, containing these 9 sections:

Wearer's basic profile (including 1.1 Weekly rhythm: the nature of my schedule from Monday through the weekend)
Designers, brand aesthetics, and frequently worn brand references
Style keywords
Color plan (base colors + accent colors + what to avoid)
Fit and silhouette preferences
Styles to absolutely avoid
Occasion ratio (5 slots, corresponding to weekdays)
Accessories
Weather adjustment rules (tiered by temperature + what shoes to switch to when it rains)
Start by interviewing me with 6 questions. Wait until I've answered before writing the file based on my responses. Do not make up content on your own.
