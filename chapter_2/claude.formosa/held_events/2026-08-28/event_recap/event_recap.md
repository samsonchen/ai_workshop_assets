claude.formosa 2026/8/28 活動摘要

謝謝大家參加上週五的 claude.formosa 的八月份 AI 小聚。

這次有三位講者以及一場特別的對談。

第一位講者 Jennifer 蔡珍珍是慈濟大學教育研究所一年級的碩士生，她也是印尼籍的留學生。由於 Jennifer 並不是中文母語，因此在花蓮生活就大量的依賴 AI 做為她的生活助理。這次她也跟大家展示出她是怎麼用 AI 在她日常生活的大小事來幫忙她，舉凡超市看到不認得的菜、購物的夥伴，到旅遊指南，全都靠生成式 AI 一手幫忙。她也順便讓大家認識了一下目前印尼的主要政界人物。

第二位講者是第二次分享的 Edward Kennedy。上一次他跟大家說明了 Skill，這一次則是跟大家詳細介紹了 Hermes Agent，包含 Agent 的作用，可以用哪些 VPS 以及模型來讓 Hermes Agent 運作。
他的簡報資料在此分享給大家：
https://latentwill.com/presentations/
裡面也包含了上一次的分享資料，沒收到（或沒收藏到）的夥伴可以趁機再收藏一次。

第三位講者 Ethin 江則分享了從他想要自己做個遊戲，一路到幫忙別人解決問題而踏上了 Hermes Agent 與 Claude Code 這條路。他的分享資料也透過這個 email 分享給大家。

第四場則是我們第一次嘗試舉辦的對談活動，除了 Sophie 與我外，特別請了在軟體產業已經 19 年的資深從業人員 L 哥一起來談談後 AI 時代的角色重塑問題，也創下了我們在雲基地活動弄到最晚散場的一次，在此特別感謝縣府工作人員閔恩的大力幫忙。

在這裡再次召集分享者，只要你有用到 AI 做任何事，我們都歡迎你來我們 AI 小聚分享，不論這件事在哪一行哪一業，或是到底有多複雜，誠摯歡迎您加入分享者行列。

claude.formosa 2026/8/28 Event Recap

Thank you all for joining last Friday's claude.formosa August AI Meetup.

This session featured three speakers and a special panel discussion.

The first speaker, Jennifer Tsai, is a first-year master's student at the Institute of Education, Tzu Chi University, and an international student from Indonesia. Since Chinese is not her native language, she relies heavily on AI as her daily personal assistant while living in Hualien. This time, she demonstrated how she uses AI to assist with every aspect of her daily life—ranging from identifying unfamiliar vegetables at the supermarket and acting as a shopping partner to serving as a travel guide—all powered by generative AI. She also took the opportunity to introduce some of the major political figures in Indonesia.

Our second speaker was Edward Kennedy, sharing with us for the second time. Last time, he explained Skills, and this time, he gave a detailed introduction to Hermes Agent, including its functionality, as well as the VPS environments and models that can be used to run it. Here is the link to his presentation slides:
https://latentwill.com/presentations/
It also includes the presentation materials from the previous session, so anyone who missed out can grab a copy now.

The third speaker, Ethin Chiang, shared his journey from initially wanting to build his own game to helping others solve problems, which led him down the path of Hermes Agent and Claude Code. His presentation materials are also shared with everyone via this email.

The fourth session was our first attempt at hosting a panel discussion. In addition to Sophie and myself, we invited Mr. L, a 19-year veteran in the software industry, to discuss the reshaping of roles in the post-AI era. This session ended up being our late-night record for events at the Cloud Hub. Special thanks to Min-En from the county government staff for his tremendous support.

We are once again calling for speakers! As long as you use AI for anything, we welcome you to share at our AI Meetup—no matter what industry you are in or how simple-or-complex the use case might be. We sincerely welcome you to join our lineup of speakers.

Since September coincides with the Mid-Autumn Festival long holiday, our September event will be paused. Our next event will take place on October 30, 2026. We look forward to seeing everyone there!
